Data controls

Delete your JaTrail data

You can delete individual trails or permanently remove all of your data.

Delete data from the Android app

  1. Open Trail history.
  2. Select the trail you want to remove.
  3. Tap the red Delete button and confirm.

If a trail is currently recording, stop or finish the recording first.

To remove all JaTrail data stored on your phone, open Android Settings → Apps → JaTrail → Storage & cache → Clear storage. You can also uninstall JaTrail. Android menu names may vary by device.

Deleting local Android data does not delete trails already uploaded to a server.

Delete a trail from the JaTrail website

  1. Sign in and open the trail.
  2. Select Edit trail.
  3. Select Delete trail and confirm.

This permanently deletes the trail, its GPS points, and its photos from that server.

Delete your server account and all data

  1. Sign in and open Account.
  2. Find Delete account.
  3. Enter your current password and select Delete account and all trails.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

This permanently removes your account and its trails, GPS points, photos, and group data. This action cannot be undone.

Need help?

For the official JaTrail service, contact support@jatrail.com. If you use another or self-hosted JaTrail server, contact that server's operator.
