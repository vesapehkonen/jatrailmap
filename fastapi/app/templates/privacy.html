JaTrail Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

JaTrail is an application for recording GPS trails, attaching photos to trails, and viewing and sharing trail information.

Information JaTrail Uses

JaTrail may process information that you provide or create while using the service, including:

  • Account information, such as your username and optional profile information.
  • GPS location data recorded when you choose to record a trail.
  • Photos that you choose to attach to and upload with a trail.
  • Trail information such as names, descriptions, distance, time, and location information.

JaTrail uses this information to provide its trail recording, storage, mapping, and sharing features.

Location Data

The Android application accesses your device's location when recording a trail. Location information is used to create the GPS track associated with your trail.

JaTrail does not record a trail unless you start the trail-recording functionality.

Photos

You may choose to take or attach photos to your trails. Photos that you upload are stored with your trail information on the selected JaTrail server.

Sharing

You control the sharing level of your trails and photos. Depending on the options you select, content may be private, shared with a group, or publicly accessible.

Do not make a trail or photo public if it contains information that you do not want others to see.

Servers and Self-Hosting

The JaTrail Android application can connect to a configurable JaTrail server.

If you use the official JaTrail service, information you upload is processed and stored by that service.

If you configure the application to use a different or self-hosted server, information you upload is sent to that server. The operator of that server is responsible for its operation and data practices.

Data Security

JaTrail uses access controls to restrict access to private trail and photo information. Internet connections to public JaTrail servers should use HTTPS.

Users who configure an unencrypted HTTP server should understand that information transmitted over HTTP is not encrypted while in transit.

Data Deletion

You can manage your trails and account information through JaTrail. If you need assistance with deletion of information associated with the JaTrail service, contact us at the address below.

Children

JaTrail is not specifically designed for children, and we do not knowingly operate the service for the purpose of collecting personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when JaTrail's features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning JaTrail, contact:

support@jatrail.com
